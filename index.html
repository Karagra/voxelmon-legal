VoxelMon and VoxelMon Demo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

VoxelMon and VoxelMon Demo ("the game") are developed and published by K-Gaming Network ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. The game has no accounts of its own and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not send any information to us.

Your saves stay on your device

Your worlds, characters, creatures, settings and progress are saved only on your headset. On Meta Quest and PICO headsets, uninstalling the game deletes them. If the VoxelMon Demo is installed on the same headset when you first start the full game, the full game copies the Demo's save so your progress carries over. This copy happens on your headset; nothing is sent anywhere.

Store and platform services

On the Meta Horizon Store, the game asks Meta's Platform service whether your account owns it, as Meta requires. We receive only a yes-or-no answer and do not store it. On Steam, the game reads your headset model from Steam to choose its default graphics settings, and tells Steam which graphics setting and render resolution it is using. Meta, PICO and Valve handle this information under their own privacy policies.

Playing with friends (full game only)

Multiplayer connects headsets directly over your local network using a join code; there is no game server. Game data, and your voice if you use voice chat, travel encrypted directly between the headsets in the session. Voice is never recorded or stored, and is sent only while you are in a session. The game uses the microphone only for voice chat, and only after you grant permission. You can turn the microphone off or use push-to-talk in the game's settings. The Demo has no multiplayer, no network access and does not use the microphone.

Reporting

Reports you make through your headset's own system tools are handled by Meta, PICO or Valve under their own policies.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
