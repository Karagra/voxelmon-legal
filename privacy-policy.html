VoxelMon and VoxelMon Demo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

VoxelMon and VoxelMon Demo ("the game") are developed and published by K-Gaming Network ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information about you.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. The game has no accounts of its own and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not send any information to us. Playing with friends over the internet uses a service run by Epic Games, described under "Playing with friends".

Your saves stay on your device

Your worlds, characters, creatures, settings and progress are saved only on your headset. On Meta Quest and PICO headsets, uninstalling the game deletes them. If the VoxelMon Demo is installed on the same headset when you first start the full game, the full game copies the Demo's save so your progress carries over. This copy happens on your headset; nothing is sent anywhere.

Store and platform services

On the Meta Horizon Store, the game asks Meta's Platform service whether your account owns it, as Meta requires. We receive only a yes-or-no answer and do not store it. On Steam, the game reads your headset model from Steam to choose its default graphics settings, and tells Steam which graphics setting and render resolution it is using. For playing with friends, the full game also reads your store account's user ID and username, as described below. Meta, PICO and Valve handle this information under their own privacy policies. Playing with friends uses Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games, Inc., as described below.

Playing with friends (full game only)

You can play with friends on your own network or over the internet. A host shares a join code. There is no game server: a session runs on the host's headset.

On the same network, game data and voice travel directly between the headsets. The headsets also contact Epic Online Services, as described below, only to confirm which account each player is.

The game uses Epic Online Services, a service of Epic Games, Inc., to confirm each player's account, to find the host from its join code and, over the internet, to connect the headsets. This happens only when you host a world or join one, never in single-player:

  • Your store account. On the Meta Horizon Store and the PICO Store, the full game asks the store for your account's user ID, a one-time proof of it, and your username, and passes them to Epic. Epic checks them with Meta or PICO and gives the game an identifier for you that is the same each time you play. It is not an Epic Games account. If your store account cannot be used, the game creates an anonymous identifier for your headset with Epic instead, which holds no name, email address or other details about you.
  • Your name in a session. Your store username is shown to the other players in the same session, above your character and in the game's Friends tab. If two players in a session have the same name, a four-digit number made from each player's identifier is shown after it. The other players' headsets keep your name only while the session lasts. Nothing about you is shown to anyone outside the session.
  • Keeping players out. A host can remove a player from their session. Any player can block another: the game then keeps that player out of games you host and silences their voice for you. Your list of blocked players (an identifier for each, and the name they had) is stored only on your headset and is deleted with the game.
  • The sign-in with Epic is stored on your headset. On Meta Quest and PICO headsets it is stored encrypted in the game's own storage and is deleted with the game. On the Steam Frame, Epic's software stores it in your user folder, where it stays until you remove it.
  • Epic receives your headset's IP address, as any internet service does, and your join code while you host.
  • Headsets connect directly to each other where your routers allow it. The other players' headsets then learn your IP address, as in any direct connection. Where a direct connection is not possible, game data and voice pass through Epic's relay servers, encrypted, and are not stored.
  • Epic's software also reports how well its service is working: a random identifier for the session, the number of requests made, how long they took and whether they succeeded.
  • Epic may process this information in the United States and in other countries where it or its service providers operate.

Epic handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacy-policy

Game data, and your voice if you use voice chat, travel encrypted between the headsets in the session. Voice is never recorded or stored, and is sent only while you are in a session. The game uses the microphone only for voice chat, and only after you grant permission. You can turn the microphone off or use push-to-talk in the game's settings, and mute any player. The Demo has no multiplayer, no network access and does not use the microphone.

Deleting your data

We do not hold any data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. What the game keeps is on your headset: uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your headset's settings, deletes your saves, settings, block list and the game's sign-in with Epic.

Epic Online Services keeps the identifier it gave the game for you. To have it deleted, email us at playkgamingnetwork@gmail.com from any address, saying which store you play on and your username there, and we will ask Epic to delete it and reply to tell you when it is done. You can also ask Epic directly, as its privacy policy describes. Deletion is free and open to everyone, wherever you live. If you play with friends again afterwards, the game creates a new identifier.

Other players' headsets may keep your identifier and name in their own block lists, on their devices, where neither we nor Epic can reach them. They contain nothing else about you.

Reporting

Reports you make through your headset's own system tools are handled by Meta, PICO or Valve under their own policies.

Children

The game does not ask anyone, including children, to enter a name, age, email address or any other details, and it has no accounts of its own, advertising or tracking. Nothing is sent to us. Playing with friends involves the store account identifier, username and IP address described above, which go to Epic only to confirm accounts and connect players; the username, which is shown only to the other players in the same session; and voice chat between the players in a session, which is never recorded. A session can be joined only with a join code shared by its host: there is no public matchmaking and no text chat.
